01 / Who we are

A flight crew of engineers from every discipline.

The official aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering.

The club

Designing, building and testing since 2012.

AeroBMSCE operates from Propel Lab–3 at BMS College of Engineering. Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft, drones, and autonomous systems.

Since we started, we’ve taken on and completed 75+ projects and produced 50+ research papers, reports and certifications — work that reflects the creativity and dedication of our members.

What we do

Four things we do every season.

Aircraft & UAV design

Competitive fixed-wing and UAV design for SAE and national contests.

Drone racing & autonomy

Racing quads and autonomous drones that navigate on their own.

Fabrication & testing

Industry-grade fabrication, ground tests and flight tests in-house.

Talent development

Learning by doing — real engineering on real deadlines.

Why it matters

The value goes beyond the trophy.

Competing at a global level teaches what a classroom can’t — and gives the companies that back us a direct line to that talent.

  • Engineers trained on real constraints
  • Talent exposure before graduation
  • Brand visibility in elite technical ecosystems

Our vision

A leading aerospace and unmanned systems research group, renowned for design excellence, innovation and competitive success.

We aim to cultivate future leaders in the aerospace and drone industry, establishing a legacy of engineering excellence that inspires students worldwide.
