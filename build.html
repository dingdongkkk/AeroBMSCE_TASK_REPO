Research & development

What we’re working on next.

Ongoing and upcoming initiatives. 100% of R&D and technical fabrication for our current flight systems is subsidised by the R&D department at BMS College of Engineering.

  • 01

    Indigenous Flight Controller

    End-to-end hardware and firmware design.

  • 02

    Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)

    High-efficiency propulsion electronics.

  • 03

    GPS-Denied Navigation

    Vision- and sensor-based autonomous navigation.

  • 04

    Surveillance Aircraft

    Re-configurable multi-mission fixed-wing UAV with convertible propulsion and an integrated modular payload bay.

  • 05

    Confidential Defence Project

    In collaboration with DRDO — details restricted.

  • 06

    Morphing Wingtip Mechanisms

    Adaptive aerodynamics for performance optimisation.

  • 07

    Deployable Micro-Fins

    Directional stability enhancement — active stability control for UAV platforms.
